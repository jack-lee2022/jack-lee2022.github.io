在使用Orcad進行pcb的Layout前, 需確定毎個元件都有其footprint,

要建立客制化的footprint, 有兩個事情要做:

1. 建立Pad: 可使用Padstacks

[![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/-CCq0zyAFNlQ/T8wYh8e2WpI/AAAAAAAABdQ/hU6f1ydKHc4/s800/padstack01.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/PZWvXRmmmEclOnfz2hOVzofZqKMDhwjUV72UAlloJEU?feat=embedwebsite)

2. 建立所有 pad的位置: 可使用footprints

[![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/-YZ2kSiZDsHA/T8wYh9qAiVI/AAAAAAAABdU/GYFcXWPDDVk/s800/footprint01.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/Z_rfTPPueR4u77CbB-gMKIfZqKMDhwjUV72UAlloJEU?feat=embedwebsite)

這裡先介紹Padstacks,

 在pcb版中有許多的layer需要定義, Padstacks變是一個Pad再各層中的定義:

[![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/-ln85O4GcQqg/T8wWbb4LS4I/AAAAAAAABc8/pzuTVzcdIQ8/s640/layer%2520name1.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/puoB2itVh-qa_WK2Ie4juYfZqKMDhwjUV72UAlloJEU?feat=embedwebsite)

舉例來說,

如果是SMD封裝,  Pad不需要穿洞通過只個洞OCB板, 只在Top層做定義:

[![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/-zja1MDG3Lfo/T8waQwOMWyI/AAAAAAAABdw/r_HhKH4kXlA/s640/padstack02.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/io1byy59KtTWpvsAvNwdSYfZqKMDhwjUV72UAlloJEU?feat=embedwebsite)

其中.

```
TOP = Top layer

SMTOP = Soldermask Top

SPTOP = Solder paste Top

ASYTOP = Assembly Top
```

如果是DIP 封裝又是不同的定義,

例如:

[![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/-5xd5VMGy_Xg/T8wcKaYpLoI/AAAAAAAABeE/aH5phQHRgUQ/s640/padstack03.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/C8Q63mpx6j1bDHUccG_c0ofZqKMDhwjUV72UAlloJEU?feat=embedwebsite)

hint:

Thru-Hole – TOP, BOTTOM, INNER, PLANE, SMTOP, SMBOT, DRLDWG, DRILL  
Surface Mount – TOP, SMTOP, SPTOP
