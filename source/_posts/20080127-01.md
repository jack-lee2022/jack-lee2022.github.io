SignalExpress , 雖然很久之前聽過 ,   
好像是不用寫程式 , 只要作一些設定 , 選一些選項 ,   
就可以完成LabVIEW程式可以達到的資料處理功能 ,   
最近又聽人提起 , 所以就想說來嘗試一下 ,  
  
1. 連結USB 6009 DAQ卡到電腦後, DAQ monitor會偵測到裝置, 並彈出視窗 , 選取第一個選項 "Begin a measurement with This Device Using NI LabVIEW SignalExpress"  
[![](https://lh6.google.com/labview.net/R5u_aISSKvI/AAAAAAAAAJM/2g_QsqngBnE/s800/0001.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/Signalexpress_daq/photo#5159928253704710898)  
  
2. 接著是SinalExpress的視窗 , 是採用Add Step的方式加入不同的功能 , 我們第一步是將USB 6009的訊號擷取進來  
[![](https://lh4.google.com/labview.net/R5u_aoSSKwI/AAAAAAAAAJU/IaXavqU6fbI/s800/0002.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/Signalexpress_daq/photo#5159928262294645506)  
  
3. 選擇Acquire Signal-->DAQmx Acquire-->Voltage  
[![](https://lh5.google.com/labview.net/R5u_a4SSKxI/AAAAAAAAAJc/fUH5FLV8HLA/s800/0003.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/Signalexpress_daq/photo#5159928266589612818)  
  
4. 選擇Dev1(USB-6009)-->ai0  
[![](https://lh6.google.com/labview.net/R5u_bISSKyI/AAAAAAAAAJk/Bwz9vsUGQBw/s800/0004.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/Signalexpress_daq/photo#5159928270884580130)  
  
5. 在SignalExpress中會出現DAQ資料擷取的參數設定 , 圖形顯示等畫面 , 按左上角的RUN後就可以執行資料擷取的動作 , 並動態顯示在圖形視窗上 ,    
[![](https://lh4.google.com/labview.net/R5u_boSSKzI/AAAAAAAAAJs/TEXnpIUhQgc/s800/0008.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/Signalexpress_daq/photo#5159928279474514738)  
  
6. 有趣的功能是 , SugnalExpress還有將在SugnalExpress中加入的步驟變成LabVIEW程式碼的功能 , 在Tool-->Generate Code-->LabVIEW Diagram  
![](https://lh5.google.com/labview.net/R5u_b4SSK0I/AAAAAAAAAJ0/kaFL7EcHi7Y/s800/0006.JPG)  
  
  
7. 轉換中...........  
[![](https://lh6.google.com/labview.net/R5u_cISSK1I/AAAAAAAAAJ8/Z5_nf68ayOg/s800/0007.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/Signalexpress_daq/photo#5159928288064449362)  
  
8. 選擇儲存路徑及檔名  
[![](https://lh3.google.com/labview.net/R5u_cYSSK2I/AAAAAAAAAKE/DsQXb-ojRUA/s800/0010.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/Signalexpress_daq/photo#5159928292359416674)  
  
9. 產生可執行的LabVIEW程式  
[![](https://lh5.google.com/labview.net/R5u_Z4SSKuI/AAAAAAAAAJE/KCPFqerCzkg/s800/011.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/Signalexpress_daq/photo#5159928249409743586)
