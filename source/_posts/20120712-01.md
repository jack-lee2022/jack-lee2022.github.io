在完成PCB Layout後, 接下來是奧送去給PCB板廠製造,

在這個時候需要產生Gerber檔,

首先, 在File-->ＣＡＭ　Ｍanager

[![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/-Q5SU8-qKJ-0/T_4Cj14nJFI/AAAAAAAABhs/9HU-d8SxKLI/s400/gb001.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/XY_GQOyZjEegsJcQAlbxUYrCraxliwG5-hw75kJZpZE?feat=embedwebsite)

產生Ｏutput Wizard視窗, 按Next,

[![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/-U3JgZPy5drY/T_4CjzSJIYI/AAAAAAAABho/462ZGzdxsmY/s400/gb002.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/dgDMrmNVhutbkI-65kb3porCraxliwG5-hw75kJZpZE?feat=embedwebsite)

 選擇Gerber,按Next,

[![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/-nCVGpWqZvgY/T_4ClF_3gLI/AAAAAAAABhw/WhodIfRnHLU/s400/gb003.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/Gdy66Ts9e7DtUS0WIg3hf4rCraxliwG5-hw75kJZpZE?feat=embedwebsite)

輸入檔案名稱, 按Next,

[![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/-BEbMGU655mk/T_4Clwx55bI/AAAAAAAABh8/KCaXQm-gZZ0/s400/gb004.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/ZPxVBIkurY0GJV4F636L7orCraxliwG5-hw75kJZpZE?feat=embedwebsite)

按Next

[![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/-EEfiMgUUMIw/T_4CnIJ2KAI/AAAAAAAABiE/64qoZxKonI0/s400/gb005.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/j9F2Ma2k2io-ROItRjFgGorCraxliwG5-hw75kJZpZE?feat=embedwebsite)

選擇gerber file輸出格式的解析度, 可依ＰＣＢ板廠建議選擇,按Next

[![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/-pW-7iY6hX-E/T_4CnJK6Z1I/AAAAAAAABiI/YHs0BD9uUTE/s400/gb006.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/-47Ubnmhp9SrKRQe6b-I8YrCraxliwG5-hw75kJZpZE?feat=embedwebsite)

 勾選要產生Gerber檔的圖層

[![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/-FI2-YwSiHNo/T_4Cr7XR2CI/AAAAAAAABig/EUQEwYSCTvk/s400/gb007.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/mWZLN6eCodGa4OeQLcepd4rCraxliwG5-hw75kJZpZE?feat=embedwebsite)

保留設定, 按Next,

[![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/-_zrSfjKZEYc/T_4Cpjs-cKI/AAAAAAAABiU/arjzVi1Vs2c/s400/gb008.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/Ej8T530uZxWLh589bhkwxIrCraxliwG5-hw75kJZpZE?feat=embedwebsite)

保留設定, 按Next,

[![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/-6tXpEkAN6TU/T_4Cr13FEDI/AAAAAAAABic/Yr8YyphY3n4/s400/gb009.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/1T3Kb2FQFPH-5wMyq1CFd4rCraxliwG5-hw75kJZpZE?feat=embedwebsite)

保留設定, 按Next,

[![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/-yv8Ve88b_ko/T_4CrxzIeLI/AAAAAAAABik/pWGu-SKCxls/s400/gb010.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/HO4EFlSyNwb2DYevzxj8MorCraxliwG5-hw75kJZpZE?feat=embedwebsite)

[![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/-0qnRqP1JGVo/T_4CstS1cGI/AAAAAAAABio/wqe234q3lvA/s400/gb011.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/WzXigKbdQOhbctRxX_dkm4rCraxliwG5-hw75kJZpZE?feat=embedwebsite)
