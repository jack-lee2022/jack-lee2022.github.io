安裝MPLAB IDE 8.10後，可以使用HITECH的C編譯器開發PIC16系列MCU，

首先要建立一個專案，

專案建立流程如下：

1. 使用Project->Project Wizard

[![](https://lh3.ggpht.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/SVNEsYlav1I/AAAAAAAAA_s/msKFno540nA/s800/Snap1.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/ktqg7tzQTPc-kXLhZumYFQ?authkey=DInZXcn8wQg&feat=embedwebsite)

2. Next

[![](https://lh4.ggpht.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/SVNEszgZoKI/AAAAAAAAA_0/Vkodo62B-N8/s800/Snap2.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/oionfdiX_wewGKqHs88IfQ?authkey=DInZXcn8wQg&feat=embedwebsite)

3. 選擇MCU種類

[![](https://lh3.ggpht.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/SVNEtROOEQI/AAAAAAAAA_8/gIrpoi77HW8/s800/Snap3.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/e2eathWfFI8ZeYuzINgzWA?authkey=DInZXcn8wQg&feat=embedwebsite)

4. 選擇編譯工具，包括C compiler與其路徑。

[![](https://lh6.ggpht.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/SVNEttDwiWI/AAAAAAAABAE/Y0ERmP_WLwc/s800/Snap4.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/McBh8i-TuTUXC9IdVMofOQ?authkey=DInZXcn8wQg&feat=embedwebsite)

5. 建立專案路徑與名稱，注意專案路徑不能有中文。

[![](https://lh4.ggpht.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/SVNEt5WlOrI/AAAAAAAABAM/SNby6ohNI64/s800/Snap5.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/ytoE4MHMQiJcCKylYbHRVg?authkey=DInZXcn8wQg&feat=embedwebsite)

6. 如果有寫好的C檔案在此步驟可以加入，或按下一步以後再加入亦可。

[![](https://lh6.ggpht.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/SVNEuS_d9aI/AAAAAAAABAU/o_Ru0WVS0ts/s800/Snap6.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/YFcJoAlXuBV5Cvp-5AvmuA?authkey=DInZXcn8wQg&feat=embedwebsite)

7. 完成專案建立。

[![](https://lh5.ggpht.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/SVNEusoTonI/AAAAAAAABAc/knMF6wD--KA/s800/Snap7.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/8sRrsbFp8pPjyLndZpAzFQ?authkey=DInZXcn8wQg&feat=embedwebsite)

8. 最後在專案管理視窗加入c檔案與h檔案。

[![](https://lh3.ggpht.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/SVNEu90sPgI/AAAAAAAABAk/-v-B8jxyOrI/s800/Snap8.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/oZuLh7EUc3QUq3FYVnUWZw?authkey=DInZXcn8wQg&feat=embedwebsite)
