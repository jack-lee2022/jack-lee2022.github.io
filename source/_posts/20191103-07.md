Read A0, A1 and A15 analog input and transfer to voltage value. in Range function check if voltage is in the right range.  
  
讀出A0, A1和A15的類比訊號，轉換成電壓訊號，  
然後再用inRange函式判斷是否有在正確的範圍內。
