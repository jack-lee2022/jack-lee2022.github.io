Protel 99SE本身有很多的電路圖元件資料庫，  
在畫電路圖時大部分的電子元件都夠用，  
不過由於電子元件推出的速度頗快，有些元件的接腳會有改變，  
所以有時還是要自己繪製新元件。  
  
試用軟體 : Protel 99 SE  
  
步驟 :   
  
1. 在元件庫中隨便選一個元件，然後Edit  
[![](https://lh5.ggpht.com/labview.net/SI0oqaBO7WI/AAAAAAAAArY/5pWuOxyGfAY/s800/001.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/ProtelNewComponent/photo?authkey=OMia_HzVuao#5227879451451125090)  
  
2. 出現元件庫的編輯視窗後，在Tool-->New Component 建立新元件，  
[![](https://lh3.ggpht.com/labview.net/SI0oq-CKI1I/AAAAAAAAArg/N5Ll551E8Ug/s800/002.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/ProtelNewComponent/photo?authkey=OMia_HzVuao#5227879461118681938)  
  
3.輸入新元件的名稱，  
[![](https://lh3.ggpht.com/labview.net/SI0orboIsXI/AAAAAAAAArw/yuJfyGi5JbU/s800/004.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/ProtelNewComponent/photo?authkey=OMia_HzVuao#5227879469062599026)  
  
4. 找出所要繪製的新元件的datasheet，了解其接腳與用途，  
[![](https://lh4.ggpht.com/labview.net/SI0oqzXTuHI/AAAAAAAAAro/ugS6iHHDoAQ/s800/003.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/ProtelNewComponent/photo?authkey=OMia_HzVuao#5227879458254600306)  
  
5. 在這個新元件的繪製所需要使用的工具有: 繪製矩形與接線  
[![](https://lh6.ggpht.com/labview.net/SI0orpJGhJI/AAAAAAAAAr4/SgfziQtN2bQ/s800/005.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/ProtelNewComponent/photo?authkey=OMia_HzVuao#5227879472690529426)  
  
6. 完成初步的繪製，在接腳上按右鍵點兩下，即出現接腳編輯視窗  
[![](https://lh4.ggpht.com/labview.net/SI0ornlsYkI/AAAAAAAAAsA/UPd3ltJzs8k/s800/006.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/ProtelNewComponent/photo?authkey=OMia_HzVuao#5227879472273580610)  
  
7. 輸入接腳名稱與編號，  
[![](https://lh6.ggpht.com/labview.net/SI0orwxxOZI/AAAAAAAAAsI/Ym7rB5RIQwc/s800/008.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/ProtelNewComponent/photo?authkey=OMia_HzVuao#5227879474740148626)  
  
8. 完成接腳的編輯後，調整最適合的元件大小，  
[![](https://lh6.ggpht.com/labview.net/SI0osFbEA4I/AAAAAAAAAsQ/w1fGEkD9mDc/s800/009.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/ProtelNewComponent/photo?authkey=OMia_HzVuao#5227879480282055554)  
  
9. 接著按"Description" 編輯元件的文字部分，在Default輸入U?，這是元件在電路圖被自動編號時的默認值，Description則輸入對該元件的功能描述，  
[![](https://lh4.ggpht.com/labview.net/SI0osTNdFmI/AAAAAAAAAsY/2CpTmaCwyIg/s800/010.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/ProtelNewComponent/photo?authkey=OMia_HzVuao#5227879483983074914)  
  
10. 接者選擇"Place" 將新元件放置在電路圖上，  
[![](https://lh6.ggpht.com/labview.net/SI0osivSkoI/AAAAAAAAAsg/Yo14Hdln5DA/s800/011.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/ProtelNewComponent/photo?authkey=OMia_HzVuao#5227879488151523970)  
  
11. 如此就完成一個新元件的建立工作。  
[![](https://lh5.ggpht.com/labview.net/SI0os5qojeI/AAAAAAAAAso/2LoMoNkjKLo/s800/012.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/ProtelNewComponent/photo?authkey=OMia_HzVuao#5227879494306008546)
