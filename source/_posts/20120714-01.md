建立好Gerber檔之後,

接著要建立NC Drill檔,

在建好Gerber file的空白處按右鍵,

出現選單, 選擇Insert NC Drill,

[![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/-rsd8u1gPm58/T_4CwFP2HEI/AAAAAAAABjE/HRPDYmCWkqg/s400/gb013.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/kdJSfvjdr2M1V9C9tW-ETYrCraxliwG5-hw75kJZpZE?feat=embedwebsite)

選擇公制或英制, 以及解析度,

[![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/-fMHt4QkK4wQ/T_4CvNERqlI/AAAAAAAABjA/8EEjVQZonzs/s400/gb014.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/KSnjW1cIGdLbyl64nguPyIrCraxliwG5-hw75kJZpZE?feat=embedwebsite)

這樣就完成NC Drill檔的設定,

同樣也可以按右鍵, Insert BOM,

接著設定BOM的輸出參數,

[![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/-FQpYzg9-efc/T_4CwU8jcoI/AAAAAAAABjI/68xua0wjqyc/s400/gb015.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/QLwZGuRa4ppBcZ2d4IklL4rCraxliwG5-hw75kJZpZE?feat=embedwebsite)

這樣就完成BOM輸出的設定

也可以再Insert Pick and Place, 給之後PCB打件使用,

[![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/-Obcw5yRBam8/T_4CyiBHoxI/AAAAAAAABjc/2zwZcxOQ64s/s400/gb016.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/ynrFJyQYXh5itrRdgvIz-4rCraxliwG5-hw75kJZpZE?feat=embedwebsite)

所有想要輸出的檔案都決定後,

接著就是要將檔案輸出,

在空白處按右鍵, 選擇Generate CAM Files,

[![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/-lbIqy4q3XBo/T_4CyvZQByI/AAAAAAAABjg/aLp1ug5Gzdc/s400/gb017.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/t-StnFhdxXhZvzbwK3Yv-4rCraxliwG5-hw75kJZpZE?feat=embedwebsite)

在左側的 Documents資料夾中出現CAM for uammeter的資料夾, 裡面有之前設定好的檔案,

[![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/-3AKo2PYWRiU/T_4CzxsoZkI/AAAAAAAABjs/Pg17ZNUIlmk/s400/gb018.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/f5gLQuMaWOD1jgk16nWVlIrCraxliwG5-hw75kJZpZE?feat=embedwebsite)

最後,在CAM for uammeter的資料夾上按右鍵, 在選單中選Export,

[![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/-0vknjlMwazc/T_4C1FZSJzI/AAAAAAAABj8/8WzdrgPb-MM/s400/gb019.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/SzVeVSwGdAlRT-47WUap64rCraxliwG5-hw75kJZpZE?feat=embedwebsite)

選擇要輸出的資料夾,

[![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/-OcikOWewu3A/T_4C1CVc-wI/AAAAAAAABjw/uxMIpNzoscM/s400/gb020.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/uZdCeI4p9KkCmJ3Az5F9SorCraxliwG5-hw75kJZpZE?feat=embedwebsite)

看一下在CAM輸出資料中的檔案, 完成輸出動作~~~

[![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/-A8FHMaa8ico/T_4C2tc2irI/AAAAAAAABkE/mxsaD76bsR0/s400/gb021.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/-SG-y6apzCK8ByZPvZeW9YrCraxliwG5-hw75kJZpZE?feat=embedwebsite)
