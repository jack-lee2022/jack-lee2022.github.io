本篇文章使用Sound and Vibration toolkit的Assistant來快速建立聲音(或聲壓Sound Pressure )的量測,

在使用Sound and Vibration Assistant前，請先將量測用的麥克風與資料擷取模組(如NI 9234)接好，

然後開啟Sound and Vibration Assistant

[![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/-i0UmovELl00/To2UTAt6l3I/AAAAAAAABUs/sA2E-I6qLr4/s400/sv000.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/HuaHqzgGAguRwkEZ7wWV5WT9B5zwy_p0w8dpM7dthHg?feat=embedwebsite)

進到Assistant畫面後，要Add Step,

[![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/-HiUopcx76c0/To2TtrTEfSI/AAAAAAAABUI/MiVCsA2FWX0/s400/sv001.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/xRjEZiElWHluUsptGyk6zGT9B5zwy_p0w8dpM7dthHg?feat=embedwebsite)

選擇Acquire Signals --> Sound Pressure

[![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/-WVdJOuxxhSY/To2TtpH4HhI/AAAAAAAABUE/MjTQCMN7yhE/s400/sv002.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/vT5tgnxcoQLM77339RwX12T9B5zwy_p0w8dpM7dthHg?feat=embedwebsite)

選擇硬體的IO (接麥克風的IO接腳)

[![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/-tmtzhHA1j8o/To2Tteca8NI/AAAAAAAABUA/aSs00r22IqQ/s400/sv003.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/tMH1ewrglCjW6zGphzL4UmT9B5zwy_p0w8dpM7dthHg?feat=embedwebsite)

輸入麥克風的校正參數 (每個麥克風出廠時都有自己的校正參數)，然後按左上角的Run

[![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/-GS7SkyJyvhM/To2TuIXieYI/AAAAAAAABUM/5jxQQNHQ6to/s400/sv004.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/Z7Ci24yNPcc9OEyMF4ZJemT9B5zwy_p0w8dpM7dthHg?feat=embedwebsite)

我們的可以量測到麥克風的時域(Time domain)訊號,

[![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/-XElLTB7Wdyo/To2TuV15NII/AAAAAAAABUQ/FuBRcwoxI3c/s400/sv005.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/5GXPuHXXnxHQ3MLEFS44jGT9B5zwy_p0w8dpM7dthHg?feat=embedwebsite)

但是我們經常是對頻域訊號感到有興趣，所以Sound and Vibration toolkit也提供頻域的分析，按Add Step, 選Analysis --> Frequency Domain Measurement --> Sound Spectrum,

[![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/-wqowNfWLKrk/To2Tug-i2eI/AAAAAAAABUU/mzxSThGi9c4/s400/sv006.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/iQ37EqtUk7nK0F1HF8i1U2T9B5zwy_p0w8dpM7dthHg?feat=embedwebsite)

設定完畢即會出現聲音的頻譜(Sound Spectrum), 可以分析Noise在不同頻率的強弱變化。

[![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/-6t-eRgjunL4/To2Tu010nuI/AAAAAAAABUY/qgsIcpUnz3s/s400/sv007.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/Ba6bUdNYeSwNse-TCwk-6GT9B5zwy_p0w8dpM7dthHg?feat=embedwebsite)

以上是簡單的聲壓訊號量測的介紹 ^^
