為了在Arduino上開發ESP32的開發版，我們需要在Arduino IDE上裝ES32 Add-On，

### 安裝Arduino IDE1.8.9

1. 到[https://www.arduino.cc/en/Main/Donate](https://draft.blogger.com/u/2/1.%20%E5%AE%89%E8%A3%9DArduino%20IDE%201.8.9%20%20%20https://www.arduino.cc/en/Main/Donate%20%E9%81%B8just%20download) 下載Arduino IDE，選just download即可。

2. 安裝ESP32 Add-On

在Arduino IDE中，檔案-->偏好設定，再額外的開發版管理員網址中輸入:

**https://dl.espressif.com/dl/package\_esp32\_index.json**

按確定。

[![](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-QSUf60cM94A/XOkDqb5tiaI/AAAAAAAALlE/ssloI476xnMbM6-kc_fGXEYiYKvSiQaswCLcBGAs/s400/arduino007.jpg)](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-QSUf60cM94A/XOkDqb5tiaI/AAAAAAAALlE/ssloI476xnMbM6-kc_fGXEYiYKvSiQaswCLcBGAs/s1600/arduino007.jpg)

  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
在Arduino IDE中，工具-->開發版-->開發版管理員，搜尋 ESP32，  
然後安裝**ESP32 by Espressif Systems**  
[![](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-aJBCR5sgONo/XOkDqU35aNI/AAAAAAAALlA/0CQ9XjxS6u0KmxrOceO5N5aaddZYr3ZvQCLcBGAs/s400/arduino008.jpg)](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-aJBCR5sgONo/XOkDqU35aNI/AAAAAAAALlA/0CQ9XjxS6u0KmxrOceO5N5aaddZYr3ZvQCLcBGAs/s1600/arduino008.jpg)  

選擇ESP32 Devkit開發版

[![](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-t6WyQHAfbHQ/XOkWTTXckaI/AAAAAAAALlU/87euEqwLcpsRKlLQZLxID3VrXcmdgyorQCLcBGAs/s400/arduino010.jpg)](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-t6WyQHAfbHQ/XOkWTTXckaI/AAAAAAAALlU/87euEqwLcpsRKlLQZLxID3VrXcmdgyorQCLcBGAs/s1600/arduino010.jpg)

開啟範例WIFI scan

[![](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-_OL1-Kk35ZQ/XOkeCsU35FI/AAAAAAAALlo/zgNMik-lDXYs0ey2J8-Jr9DI6SDgWxYAQCLcBGAs/s400/arduino012.jpg)](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-_OL1-Kk35ZQ/XOkeCsU35FI/AAAAAAAALlo/zgNMik-lDXYs0ey2J8-Jr9DI6SDgWxYAQCLcBGAs/s1600/arduino012.jpg)
