由於想要學習Python的Web應用程式Django，因此選擇Pycharm做為開發工具。

本片會接紹Pycharm的安裝，以及第一個簡單的Django專案。

1. 下載pycharm <https://www.jetbrains.com/pycharm/download/#section=windows>

[![](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-CGtC1KwpsD0/XOi7pm6B8dI/AAAAAAAALhs/VFxmuOv1N5UJepa0chRvYzVk-aHbM_iBwCLcBGAs/s400/pycharm001.png)](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-CGtC1KwpsD0/XOi7pm6B8dI/AAAAAAAALhs/VFxmuOv1N5UJepa0chRvYzVk-aHbM_iBwCLcBGAs/s1600/pycharm001.png)

2. 開始安裝Pycharm

[![](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-CchLdCFyOGA/XOi9LlRDdfI/AAAAAAAALh4/fgihPd-Xle07x94p5m-vZnYbq3Eyqfl4ACLcBGAs/s400/win%2Bpycharm002.jpg)](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-CchLdCFyOGA/XOi9LlRDdfI/AAAAAAAALh4/fgihPd-Xle07x94p5m-vZnYbq3Eyqfl4ACLcBGAs/s1600/win%2Bpycharm002.jpg)

3. 如果有遇到找不到exe檔的問題: FileNotFoundError: [Errno 2] No such file or directory: 'D:\\Python37\\lib\\venv\\scripts\\nt\\python\_d.exe'

將python\_d.exe和pythonw\_d.exe複製到該資料夾。

4. 新建專案

[![](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-iJkMbTvBNUc/XOi-GB4zShI/AAAAAAAALiE/LH7QWQ-KhXwXsatfX-0jIV_w62k6hLgJQCLcBGAs/s400/win%2Bpyxharm003.jpg)](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-iJkMbTvBNUc/XOi-GB4zShI/AAAAAAAALiE/LH7QWQ-KhXwXsatfX-0jIV_w62k6hLgJQCLcBGAs/s1600/win%2Bpyxharm003.jpg)

4. 選擇Django，專案為firstweb

[![](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-R6QVGzzrV00/XOi-53pPg-I/AAAAAAAALiQ/TP-NM5M-3l0YJJiIqdZaUBo4Keo_iH16ACLcBGAs/s400/win%2Bpycharm004.jpg)](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-R6QVGzzrV00/XOi-53pPg-I/AAAAAAAALiQ/TP-NM5M-3l0YJJiIqdZaUBo4Keo_iH16ACLcBGAs/s1600/win%2Bpycharm004.jpg)

5.  建立專案中

[![](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-9md-Ng0inxI/XOi_kLFr1rI/AAAAAAAALiY/3iG_xctt64ssBCgJUNFA3FGJFVZ8q2ypwCLcBGAs/s400/win%2Boycharm005.jpg)](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-9md-Ng0inxI/XOi_kLFr1rI/AAAAAAAALiY/3iG_xctt64ssBCgJUNFA3FGJFVZ8q2ypwCLcBGAs/s1600/win%2Boycharm005.jpg)

6. 按執行鍵，執行專案，等同python manage.py runserver

[![](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-O96k5fy5coU/XOjAqEZhylI/AAAAAAAALik/eWTF_XLxIRcOVsXjX0Ry0v183a-_7-qVACLcBGAs/s400/win%2Bpycharm006.jpg)](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-O96k5fy5coU/XOjAqEZhylI/AAAAAAAALik/eWTF_XLxIRcOVsXjX0Ry0v183a-_7-qVACLcBGAs/s1600/win%2Bpycharm006.jpg)

7. 在瀏覽器輸入127.0.0.1:8000，一個簡單的Django專案已經執行。

[![](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-KjiARggJNZ0/XOjBgo08EkI/AAAAAAAALiw/540g0Pq8WPUvpRHLdZgxvVIEZUYHU-QbwCLcBGAs/s400/win%2Bpycharm007.jpg)](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-KjiARggJNZ0/XOjBgo08EkI/AAAAAAAALiw/540g0Pq8WPUvpRHLdZgxvVIEZUYHU-QbwCLcBGAs/s1600/win%2Bpycharm007.jpg)
