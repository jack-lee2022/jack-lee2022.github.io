Diptrace是一套價格很便宜的電路圖繪製與PCB layout的軟體 (有許多版本可以選擇)

關於Diptrace的資訊, 可先參考：https://www.diptrace.com/

有free的試用版可供下載,

這套軟體有四個Editor: Component Editor, Pattern Editor, PCB Layout, Schematic.

我先來介紹其中的Component Editor, 用來建立電路圖中的元件,

1. 首先啟動Component Editor後, 按新增元件,

[![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/-fh-EKiHpQY4/Tub2-0Hj6hI/AAAAAAAABaM/a4jiUkIc0Z8/s400/component001.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/Xs_-UZd-zhCEux5uejNB8EgHv_ry1-0QQyHu_roef2U?feat=embedwebsite)

2. 在component properties中選擇元件種類 大小與接腳數

[![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/-nXV9ZktfSL0/Tub2_FSEuYI/AAAAAAAABaQ/cbWdUvfR9Zo/s400/component002.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/8K27mMa1nzPIzu1I6TWIrkgHv_ry1-0QQyHu_roef2U?feat=embedwebsite)

3. 每個pin按左鍵兩下, 會彈Pin properties視窗, 出分別填入Name以及Number

[![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/-ODHJCSZ25FU/Tub2_VosPmI/AAAAAAAABaU/aLK6NE9Lzb0/s400/component003.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/-BJuLIHXkvSFsecxEWOzCkgHv_ry1-0QQyHu_roef2U?feat=embedwebsite)

簡單的三個步驟就可以完成電路元件的建立了 ^^
