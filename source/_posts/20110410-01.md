我們通常在畫好電路圖後，

下一步要做的就是轉換成PCB的檔案，

在轉換之前，先要確定電路圖的元件都要有對應的PCB元件的封裝，

 以下面電路圖為例，在R1按左鍵兩下，會彈出Part視窗，

[![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/TaEc1HNeB0I/AAAAAAAABQs/W2Ht4cgqlBg/s400/annotate005.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/165iqEwU5vUEBpOr9-vPz_CSkaXWfyY191DyXXDqlak?feat=embedwebsite)

 在Attribute選項中有一個Footprint，可以填入PCB元件封裝名稱，

[![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/TaEttn7t2pI/AAAAAAAABQ4/jpDyPV_DARU/s400/BuildPCB001.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/MmiyUgj8dfL8ZevoM5TnQAR8Mkipv22De0FKHQhhn6A?feat=embedwebsite)

如填入0603，之後C1的Footprint也填入0603，J1與J2則是SIP2，

[![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/TaEttrND3tI/AAAAAAAABQ0/k9KmCT0V63k/s400/BuildPCB002.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/BQrJFc-mZCiuncjFuxtE9QR8Mkipv22De0FKHQhhn6A?feat=embedwebsite)

至於PCB元件的封裝資料要去哪裡找，這就先要在Document中先建立一個新的PCB檔案

[![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/TaEttn83gZI/AAAAAAAABQ8/Sv59tFBYeoY/s400/BuildPCB003.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/-PKIdRPyTrtOT7F1tVE00gR8Mkipv22De0FKHQhhn6A?feat=embedwebsite)

然後在PCB檔案中可以找到PCB的元件封裝庫，可以發現各式各樣的封裝，當然你也可以自己建立新的元件封裝，

[![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/TaEtuGknqAI/AAAAAAAABRA/iitG6W9jMXU/s400/BuildPCB004.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/_EnrxVZbllkNpVv4QnwXoQR8Mkipv22De0FKHQhhn6A?feat=embedwebsite)

接下來要建立電路圖元件與PCB元件的連結了，首先在Design中選Create Netlist，

[![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/TaEtuFSJb_I/AAAAAAAABRI/CCK8-fNDEX4/s400/BuildPCB005.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/5pdOUkDX2m_LpxR9iqDNAAR8Mkipv22De0FKHQhhn6A?feat=embedwebsite)

出現Netlist Creation視窗，按OK，

[![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/TaEtuAA58LI/AAAAAAAABRE/nl1HANqAyrg/s400/BuildPCB006.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/ToCsJ5xDfmghQo6E3lkvhAR8Mkipv22De0FKHQhhn6A?feat=embedwebsite)

產生.NET的檔案，

[![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/TaEtunde7YI/AAAAAAAABRM/4eMmWPtp7aw/s400/BuildPCB008.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/09NpYZNkiOD05N13gFv86wR8Mkipv22De0FKHQhhn6A?feat=embedwebsite)

接著要將電路圖轉換成PCB，在Design中選Update PCB進行轉換，

[![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/TaEtukjJM8I/AAAAAAAABRU/xem_YPC56y8/s400/BuildPCB009.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/TnY_422GFNtTSTG1g3FR6AR8Mkipv22De0FKHQhhn6A?feat=embedwebsite)

出現Update Design視窗，按Execute，

[![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/TaEt4hDXMRI/AAAAAAAABRc/NlAoskB9jIY/s400/BuildPCB010.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/FJmIHTpplo-VKCgD86QxHwR8Mkipv22De0FKHQhhn6A?feat=embedwebsite)

最後，轉換完成，我們看PCB檔，所有PCB元件都出現了。

[![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/TaEtvb-DqFI/AAAAAAAABRY/RbHlDdYm-1c/s400/BuildPCB011.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/RQ64iZJabcZWsG9JCSf4IAR8Mkipv22De0FKHQhhn6A?feat=embedwebsite)
