StopWatch是Microchip MPLAB開發環境提供的一個小工具,

可以利用軟體的模擬, 來知道一段程式碼所需要花的執行時間,

通常是利用中斷點(breakpoint)來定義所需要量測的程式碼範圍,

一般使用的方式為:

1. 在debug模式時選擇MPLAB SIM --> 軟體模擬

[![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/-RnknAa6_MCk/UQxl709PnPI/AAAAAAAABoU/iWWcyxMxt4M/s400/001.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/FwyqeG9sUKn6Ds3r4xwzkGJSKvx7XjdUjUAxOYsC4YI?feat=embedwebsite)

2. 然後選擇Debug --> StopWatch

[![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/-oGb046HJN3o/UQxl77XYQxI/AAAAAAAABoY/A6hap7R8kFI/s400/002.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/vZ8SMPr1H24O4a4A0lFUMGJSKvx7XjdUjUAxOYsC4YI?feat=embedwebsite)

3. 然後出現StopWatch視窗

[![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/-wEZf_aWCK4o/UQxnLGQEMII/AAAAAAAABpE/PCxF-i7wDAc/s400/005.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/mV8MKtwkWvtkCg1jCOsmqGJSKvx7XjdUjUAxOYsC4YI?feat=embedwebsite)

4. 接著在想知道程式碼執行時間的點上作中斷點

[![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/-ba_XNKDDdak/UQxl77iI4bI/AAAAAAAABoQ/r5xIsr5rDTE/s400/003.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/-ef94k_9WJQxDzzD5omBS2JSKvx7XjdUjUAxOYsC4YI?feat=embedwebsite)

5. 然後Build程式碼並執行, 這樣在StopWatch上便可以看到執行到中斷點所花的時間了.

[![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/-EFYlM6f-hgY/UQxl8dPGxOI/AAAAAAAABok/2D7DhKnvIEw/s400/004.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/z4E6Is1mqWKLewFREA2oT2JSKvx7XjdUjUAxOYsC4YI?feat=embedwebsite)
