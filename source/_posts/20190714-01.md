本篇簡單說明如何使用Pycharm來開發Python程式，  
  
以下是建立.py與執行的步驟:  
  
1. File-->New project  
  
[![](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-YKwUGAoCYGg/XSFxB8yZ0fI/AAAAAAAAO-k/nSKk-xhzOkEkOMHg4gJ3PZihwEqbkJzbACLcBGAs/s400/helloworld01.jpg)](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-YKwUGAoCYGg/XSFxB8yZ0fI/AAAAAAAAO-k/nSKk-xhzOkEkOMHg4gJ3PZihwEqbkJzbACLcBGAs/s1600/helloworld01.jpg)  
  
2. 在helloworld資料夾右鍵，出現下拉選單，選擇-->New-->Python File  
  
[![](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-TX3OPPcstkA/XSFxCBd2ncI/AAAAAAAAO-s/Sc4wEwPfv28shT7UdS_EZss0Wx0rPp4JwCLcBGAs/s400/helloworld02.jpg)](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-TX3OPPcstkA/XSFxCBd2ncI/AAAAAAAAO-s/Sc4wEwPfv28shT7UdS_EZss0Wx0rPp4JwCLcBGAs/s1600/helloworld02.jpg)  
  
3. 填上檔案名稱  
  
[![](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-MmbJVYDxDa4/XSFxBxQLdWI/AAAAAAAAO-o/K37aecLaZLUg0kDScXLPvN0bMyR15KOSACLcBGAs/s400/helloworld03.jpg)](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-MmbJVYDxDa4/XSFxBxQLdWI/AAAAAAAAO-o/K37aecLaZLUg0kDScXLPvN0bMyR15KOSACLcBGAs/s1600/helloworld03.jpg)  
  
4. 在程式碼中填入print("Hello World!")  
  
[![](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-C8SzqwW6SI4/XSFxCuaS6lI/AAAAAAAAO-w/-6vpvgmktccVTraJN7pHPqLmAPDuovt1wCLcBGAs/s400/helloworld04.jpg)](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-C8SzqwW6SI4/XSFxCuaS6lI/AAAAAAAAO-w/-6vpvgmktccVTraJN7pHPqLmAPDuovt1wCLcBGAs/s1600/helloworld04.jpg)  
  
5. 在helloworld.py上按右鍵，出現下拉選單，選擇Run 'helloworld'  
  
[![](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-XyMbnkz9ixs/XSFxDKDR1ZI/AAAAAAAAO-0/odUNZ19FDKI40ytxQ9a67H_AIvTBkaBRwCLcBGAs/s400/helloworld05.jpg)](https://1.bp.blogspot.com/-XyMbnkz9ixs/XSFxDKDR1ZI/AAAAAAAAO-0/odUNZ19FDKI40ytxQ9a67H_AIvTBkaBRwCLcBGAs/s1600/helloworld05.jpg)
