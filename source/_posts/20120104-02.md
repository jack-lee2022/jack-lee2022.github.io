最近試著將Protel DXP 2002的schematic (.sch)轉成Orcad的dsn檔,

 (Protel 99se的sch也是無法轉成Orcad的dsn)

找了許多網路資料, 發現要用protel DXP 2004以上的版本才可以轉換,

 (Protel 99se也可以用Protel DXP 2004開啟, 然後做轉換^^)

在Protel DXP 2004的File-->Save Project As

[![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/-mRBd8zzFNhw/TwOpCnvFLsI/AAAAAAAABa4/oVfjKxHQDSg/s400/DXP001.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/HX12NTTCUCPElncPZR3m9Xu4nOPWDR8YjhsVRJxRDmk?feat=embedwebsite)

會彈出一對話視窗,

在[存檔類型]選擇''Export Orcad Capture Design(.DSN)''

[![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/-nmaAiEYvLxA/TwOpDgSgV8I/AAAAAAAABbA/dVm-gTazRP4/s400/DXP002.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/oLMCeixyKwh49u27DPuBGXu4nOPWDR8YjhsVRJxRDmk?feat=embedwebsite)

完成轉換後, 用Orcad提供的免費viewer (Myriad Capture Viewer)查看

[![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/-PgJVRlND0NE/TwOpC3DN-UI/AAAAAAAABa8/T6u6BfBMl4w/s400/DXP003.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/C399vkaR_p0QBffVf7oIh3u4nOPWDR8YjhsVRJxRDmk?feat=embedwebsite)

雖然有完成轉換, 但元件的接腳與說明都移位了~~~~
