之前介紹了Write To Speadsheet File.vi 可以將資料轉換成字串的方式存檔，

本篇介紹如何將資料從檔案讀出，使用Read From Spreadsheet File.vi

可以從Fuctions視窗-->File I/O --> Read From Spreadsheet File.vi

[![](https://lh6.ggpht.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/TLCKTA1toUI/AAAAAAAABN0/YnrTOQ7Ipbc/s400/readdata01.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/2L6iKVjESHXZPbXyDKhilNGNvktD9gp3XQBRU3TRgXc?feat=embedwebsite)

我們來看這個vi的接腳部分，基本要使用的話只需要接file path與all rows或first row，

[![](https://lh6.ggpht.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/TLCKTUos1WI/AAAAAAAABN4/XsL4UJPe7as/s400/readdata02.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/88Ho-M5wcH-cHb2mDPSmfNGNvktD9gp3XQBRU3TRgXc?feat=embedwebsite)

在輸出格式方面有Double, Integer與String可以選，端看後續要接哪一種資料處理方式。

[![](https://lh6.ggpht.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/TLCKTm1XMnI/AAAAAAAABN8/SzgiPEovIqI/s800/readdata03.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/k9WBrHWSxVYripB8Dv5LDdGNvktD9gp3XQBRU3TRgXc?feat=embedwebsite)

 寫一個簡單的範例程式 (其實只是接線)

[![](https://lh3.ggpht.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/TLCKVDTeiMI/AAAAAAAABOM/RHKDRetEvFA/s800/readdata06.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/3qMxochMPZJQtIlO1BUNAdGNvktD9gp3XQBRU3TRgXc?feat=embedwebsite)

在Front Panel的File Path控制項要增加一個Browse Button，方便選擇要開啟哪個檔案，

[![](https://lh5.ggpht.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/TLCKTwy51uI/AAAAAAAABOA/GSBtl70Oy6c/s400/readdata04.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/y8IuwfaE90gzWscr38VNztGNvktD9gp3XQBRU3TRgXc?feat=embedwebsite)

 在File Path控制項旁就會出現Browse Button，

[![](https://lh5.ggpht.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/TLCKUOKst6I/AAAAAAAABOE/oWwT7Qx4DDI/s400/readdata05.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/gNyjfw-U0SJl3lnvS6r9NdGNvktD9gp3XQBRU3TRgXc?feat=embedwebsite)

 選擇上篇文章存檔(Write To Speadsheet File.vi )的test.txt

[![](https://lh5.ggpht.com/_AMuZNDyhqSY/TLCKU1GRuHI/AAAAAAAABOI/cVWU98kYEYA/s800/readdata07.jpg)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/nJgfJZIeKOTIrBgFKdPlTtGNvktD9gp3XQBRU3TRgXc?feat=embedwebsite)
