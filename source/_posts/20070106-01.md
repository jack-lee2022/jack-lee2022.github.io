使用軟體:  
Labview 8.2  
Vision 8.2.0 Development Module  
NI-IMAQ for USB Cameras  
  
使用硬體:  
羅技 QuickCam® Chat    
  
![](/images/pixnet/20070106-01/8c225b8fb974.jpg)  
  
  
以上Labview 8.2與Vision 8.2.0 Development Module在NI網站皆有試用版可供下載,試用時間為30天

NI-IMAQ for USB Cameras的下載網頁為<https://zone.ni.com/devzone/cda/epd/p/id/5030>,(或<ftp://ftp.ni.com/pub/devzone/epd/usb_installer_setup.exe>)  
需要先灌的軟體為:   
LabVIEW 7.0 or higher with NI Vision 7.1 or higher for the USB LabVIEW support.   
- Vision Assistant 7.1 or higher for the USB Vision Assistant plugin module.   
  
資料收集:  
<https://labview-tech.blogspot.com>  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
步驟:   
1. 安裝順序為 : Labview 8.2 -->Vision 8.2.0 Development Module-->NI-IMAQ for USB Cameras  
  
2. 安裝完成之後到 C:\Program Files\National Instruments\LabVIEW 8.2\examples\imaq\會找到imaqUSB examples  
  
3. 打開imaqUSB examples  
![](/images/pixnet/20070106-01/b5653b498a1c.jpg)  
  
4, 點選Grab.vi , 按RUN, 即可從webcam中擷取影像
