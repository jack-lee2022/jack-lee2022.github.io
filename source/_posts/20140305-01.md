你能說出你的核心能力是甚麼?

Can you figure out what is your core capability?

像律師，會計師，或顧問請教他們事情是需要付費的，

Like a lawer, accounter or consultant, you have to pay when you ask them questions.

因為他們提供了本身的核心能力，而那是他們努力得來的經驗，

Because they provide their core capability which they make a large of effort to get important experiences.

你能問到答案的，通常是簡單的事，

A free solution you can obtain usually is a easy or simple question.

有些專業的解答，是很有價值的經驗，

Some expertise solutions are from valuable experiences.

通常是不會免費的 。

They usually are not free.
