這篇文章是說明我初步在Visual  Studio 2005中撰寫簡單的QT程式，  
  
首先必須要先下載與安裝QT的兩個程式：  
1. QT4.4.0商業評估版  
2. QT ntegration for Visual Studio  
  
步驟：  
  
1. 啟動Visual Studio with QT4.4.0，建立新專案，  
[![](https://lh6.ggpht.com/labview.net/SHilh6R-iBI/AAAAAAAAApA/EJdvLO_UXo0/s800/001.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/QtVs2005/photo?authkey=teSIcqhIkgM#5222105769935013906)  
  
[![](https://lh6.ggpht.com/labview.net/SHilh0k2mpI/AAAAAAAAApI/dsqYj0W4X88/s800/002.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/QtVs2005/photo?authkey=teSIcqhIkgM#5222105768403573394)  
  
可以依自己需要選擇下一步設定，不過目前就先選擇Finish  
[![](https://lh5.ggpht.com/labview.net/SHilh1Zp3CI/AAAAAAAAApQ/ixzaQnMBOT4/s800/003.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/QtVs2005/photo?authkey=teSIcqhIkgM#5222105768625036322)  
  
2. 在Visual studio中已經以看到QT的蹤跡，  
在專案總管的方案中，Form Files出現的qttest20080712.ui檔，  
Header Files中有qttest20080712.h，  
Source Files中有main.cpp與qttest20090712.cpp，.....................  
  
[![](https://lh5.ggpht.com/labview.net/SHilh4LpNSI/AAAAAAAAApY/lLyu2fbsF5s/s800/004.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/QtVs2005/photo?authkey=teSIcqhIkgM#5222105769371579682)  
  
3. 點選qttest20080712.ui，新增一個PushButton，test屬性填入"Click Me !"  
  
[![](https://lh5.ggpht.com/labview.net/SHiliAkUTaI/AAAAAAAAApg/gZTHdRB22fs/s800/005.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/QtVs2005/photo?authkey=teSIcqhIkgM#5222105771622550946)  
在PushButton上點兩下，會在qttest20090712.cpp中出現void qttest20080712::on\_pushButton\_clicked()函數，  
將下列程式碼填入，  
    QMessageBox box(this);  
    box.setText("Hi, hit me.");  
    box.exec();  
  
其內容為當按下Click Me !的PushButtopn會啟動MessageBox函數，出現Hi, hit me.的訊息視窗。  
  
[![](https://lh5.ggpht.com/labview.net/SHiln6E1q4I/AAAAAAAAApo/kjxhxAQbIBk/s800/006.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/labview.net/QtVs2005/photo?authkey=teSIcqhIkgM#5222105872959122306)
