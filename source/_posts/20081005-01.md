有時打開Labview的VI程式時，會發生一直找不到連接的子VI，  
Sometimes you open a LabVIEW's VI, VI can't connect relative sub-VI.

可能是VI與子VI的路徑有改變的關係，  
the possible reson is the path of files  are changed,

此時要做的動作如下，

Now you have to do...

 1. 打開VI時，發現有子VI找不到，  
     When you open VI and find some VIs are missed,

[![](https://lh4.ggpht.com/labview.net/SOgFjEmvZhI/AAAAAAAAAxM/Jgbx89v74-g/s800/001.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/eMTNVLu8oCLHMevuGDsK9g?authkey=K5_7k1xY4OQ)   
  
2.  有些錯誤的子VI出現在Block Diagram中，  
     some error sub-VI appear in the Block Diagram,

[![](https://lh3.ggpht.com/labview.net/SOgFjephaPI/AAAAAAAAAxU/Xmbz8-WZQbo/s800/002.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/ukl9aEVQG8K3IO0T6K2C1w?authkey=K5_7k1xY4OQ)

3. 在其中一個錯誤的子VI按右鍵兩下，會出現遺失的VI名稱，  
    Double Click right button on error sub-VI, you can find error sub-VI's name,  
  
[![](https://lh5.ggpht.com/labview.net/SOgFke9nKBI/AAAAAAAAAx0/OOKsDbIgHSo/s800/006.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/FYFDpaY2leWFVsmy3dbGkA?authkey=K5_7k1xY4OQ)   
  
4. 在錯誤的子VI上按右鍵，選擇Select a VI...,  
   Click right button on error sub-VI and choose "Select a VI...",  
  
[![](https://lh3.ggpht.com/labview.net/SOgFjk8wwII/AAAAAAAAAxc/HGYT4nDdgkU/s800/003.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/62yqccbI-YgNHhxLgdsp1w?authkey=K5_7k1xY4OQ)   
  
4. 找到子V路徑，  
    Find sub-VI's path,

[![](https://lh3.ggpht.com/labview.net/SOgFj-t_UKI/AAAAAAAAAxk/Eqfyh-enpQ8/s800/004.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/VxtIwTgv1qyAWZezVtRVWQ?authkey=K5_7k1xY4OQ)

 5. 子V連接成功。  
     Connection succed, error sub-VI become a normal sub-VI .

[![](https://lh5.ggpht.com/labview.net/SOgFkNdUgmI/AAAAAAAAAxs/JXniwXYoPDI/s800/005.JPG)](https://picasaweb.google.com/lh/photo/GLpAh8d52iBTZlNcP54iKw?authkey=K5_7k1xY4OQ)
